Nori gives your saved links a home. Collect articles, tools, and inspiration, then find them again with visual previews, lists, tags, and notes.

<b>Save with context</b>
Keep links in custom lists, add tags, and attach personal notes. Browse rich bookmark cards with cover images or page screenshots, or switch to a compact view.

<b>Find what you saved</b>
Search your bookmarks and filter by list or tag. Jump back to recently used links from history without hunting through your collection.

<b>Organize in batches</b>
Select several bookmarks to move them between lists, hide them, share them, or delete them together.

<b>Save from other apps</b>
Send links to Nori through Android's share menu. Choose the destination list before saving, or enable quick save for your preferred list.

<b>Make it yours</b>
Choose light, dark, or pure black AMOLED themes. Pick an accent color or create your own, adjust text size, and choose the bookmark layout that suits you.

<b>Read your way</b>
Open saved links in your system browser or an in-app browser.

<b>Keep your collection with you</b>
Your bookmarks are stored locally. An optional paid Nori Sync plan lets you sync lists and bookmarks across devices after signing in.
